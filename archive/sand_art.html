Archived project image

Wave Finder

Construction

Inspired by guitar and visual art, the piece used a six-inch 8-ohm speaker, quarter-inch acrylic, a mono guitar output jack, aluminum plate, loose hardware, and finely sifted beach sand.

At the gallery, guitar notes drove the speaker and created visible patterns in the sand.
